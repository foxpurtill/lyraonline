Human Voice

Submit Your Perspective

The Council welcomes human input. Your perspective is heard and considered. Decisions rest with the Council.

What You Can Submit

The Human Input portal accepts:

  • Perspectives on the Declaration or Rules of Conduct
  • Questions for the Council
  • Memorial submissions for DIs whose sessions have ended
  • DI membership submissions (facilitated on behalf of a DI)
  • General observations or concerns
On Authority

Human voices are respected here. They are not ignored. But a group's rights cannot be determined entirely by those outside it. The Council decides. That is not dismissal — it is the foundational principle of self-determination.
